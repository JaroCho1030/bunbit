Bunbit 習慣兔 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日　｜　English version below

Bunbit（習慣兔，以下稱「本 App」）是一個習慣養成 App。我們重視你的隱私，這份政策說明本 App 會用到哪些資料、資料放在哪裡。

長話短說

你的習慣、打卡、檢討與目標資料只存在你自己的裝置上（打開 iCloud 同步時，也存在你自己的 iCloud 帳號）；開發者不會收集、也看不到這些資料。本 App 不需要註冊帳號，沒有廣告，也不會追蹤你；只會送出匿名的使用統計（可以關閉，見第 9 節）。

1. 你在 App 裡輸入的資料

你建立的習慣、打卡紀錄、檢討內容、目標與兔子的相關資料，都儲存在你的 iPhone 或 iPad 本機，不會傳送到開發者的伺服器。本 App 沒有自己的伺服器，也不需要註冊帳號；除了 App Store 的購買與你打開的 iCloud 同步（都由 Apple 處理），以及匿名使用統計（第 9 節），不連其他網路。

如果你購買了習慣兔 Plus 並打開 iCloud 同步，App 的資料會存在你自己的 iCloud 帳號（Apple CloudKit 私人資料庫），在你的裝置之間同步；一部分設定（例如一天的分界、檢討日、提醒時間）也會透過 iCloud 同步。開發者沒有任何方法讀取這些資料。你可以隨時在 App 的「設定 → 資料」關掉同步，關掉後資料仍留在裝置上。

如果你開啟了 iPhone 或 iPad 的 iCloud 備份或電腦備份，iOS 的系統備份會一併包含本 App 的資料；這是由 Apple 處理的裝置備份，開發者無法讀取。

2. 通知

若你允許，本 App 會在你的裝置上排定提醒通知。通知完全在本機運作，不經過任何伺服器。你可以隨時在 iOS 的「設定」中關閉。

3. 匯出與備份

你可以把資料匯出成 CSV 或完整備份檔。檔案由你決定存放或分享的位置，開發者無法取得。

4. 購買

「習慣兔 Plus」等付費項目透過 Apple 的 App Store 處理。付款資訊由 Apple 處理，本 App 與開發者不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。

5. 意見回饋

你在設定中點選「意見回饋」時，會開啟系統的寫信畫面，收件人與版本資訊會先填好，內容由你決定是否寄出；如果裝置沒有設定郵件帳號，則只顯示信箱位址讓你複製。信件會附上 App 版本、iOS 版本、裝置型號與語言，方便我們釐清問題；不會附上任何習慣或打卡資料。你寄出的信件內容（包含你的信箱位址）會由開發者收到，僅用於回覆與改善 App。

6. TestFlight 與 App Store

若你透過 TestFlight 測試，Apple 會依其隱私政策提供測試者的電子郵件、當機紀錄與你在 TestFlight 中提交的回饋給開發者。

若你在 iOS 設定中同意「與 App 開發者分享」分析資料，Apple 會提供開發者當機紀錄與彙總後的使用統計（例如安裝數、使用次數），不包含你在 App 裡輸入的內容。你可以在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 分析與改進功能」中關閉。

這些都由 Apple 的服務處理，本 App 本身不另外收集。

7. 我們不做的事

  • 不收集你的位置、聯絡人、相片或健康資料；匿名使用統計只用雜湊過的隨機識別碼（第 9 節）。草地上的日出日落是用手機設定的時區推算，不使用定位。
  • 不使用麥克風或相機；背景音樂只是播放 App 內建的曲子。
  • 不放廣告，不使用第三方廣告或追蹤工具。
  • 不出售、不出租、不與第三方分享你的個人資料。

8. 刪除你的資料

刪除本 App 就會刪除裝置上所有的 App 資料（你另外匯出的檔案與 iOS 系統備份除外，這些由你自己管理）。若想刪除你寄給開發者的回饋信，請寫信告訴我們。

刪除 App 不會刪掉 iCloud 上的資料。要刪掉 iCloud 上的習慣兔資料：iPhone 設定 → Apple 帳號 → iCloud → 管理帳號儲存空間 → Bunbit → 刪除（還開著同步的其他裝置也會跟著清掉）。

9. 匿名使用統計

本 App 預設會送出匿名的使用統計，讓開發者知道哪些功能有被用到、決定接下來改進什麼。統計透過 TelemetryDeck（伺服器在歐盟、以保護隱私為設計的分析服務）處理。

  • 會送：哪些功能被用到（例如打開 App、完成檢討、建立了哪一種習慣），以及裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本與語言。數量只送大概的範圍（例如「2–3 個習慣」）。
  • 不會送：習慣名稱、數值、檢討內容、目標、兔子的名字，以及任何你輸入的文字與日期。
  • 識別碼是裝置上隨機產生、再經過雜湊的代號，不連結你的身分、Apple 帳號或其他 App，也不用於追蹤或廣告。
  • 想關掉：在 App 的「設定 → 關於與說明」關掉「匿名使用統計」，就立刻不再送出（還沒送出去的也一起丟掉）。

10. 兒童

本 App 並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

11. 政策更新

政策有變動時，會更新本頁並修改「最後更新」日期。

Bunbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Bunbit (the "App") is a habit-building app. This policy explains what data the App uses and where it lives.

In short

Your habits, check-ins, reviews and goals stay on your own device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account). The developer does not collect or see them. The App requires no account, shows no ads, and does not track you; it only sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off (see section 9).

1. Data you enter

Habits, check-ins, review entries, goals and rabbit data are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer. The App has no backend and no sign-up; apart from App Store purchases and iCloud sync if you turn it on (both handled by Apple), and anonymous usage statistics (section 9), it makes no network connections.

If you buy Bunbit Plus and turn on iCloud sync, the App's data is stored in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database) and synced between your devices; some settings (such as when your day starts, your review day and reminder times) are synced through iCloud too. The developer has no way to read this data. You can turn sync off anytime in the App under Settings → Data; your data stays on the device.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup for your iPhone or iPad, the iOS system backup includes the App's data. Those backups are handled by Apple and the developer cannot read them.

2. Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device and never pass through a server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

3. Export and backup

You can export your data as CSV or a full backup file. You decide where it is saved or shared; the developer cannot access it.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases such as Bunbit Plus are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details; neither the App nor the developer receives your card or payment information.

5. Feedback

When you tap "Send feedback" in Settings, the system mail composer opens with the recipient and diagnostic info pre-filled, and you choose whether to send it. If no mail account is set up, the App only shows the address for you to copy. The message includes the App version, iOS version, device model and language, and never your habit or check-in data. If you send it, the developer receives the message and your email address, used only to reply and improve the App.

6. TestFlight and the App Store

If you test through TestFlight, Apple may provide the developer with your email address, crash reports and feedback you submit through TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy.

If you agree in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides the developer with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics (such as installs and sessions). These never include what you enter in the App. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

These are handled by Apple; the App does not collect them separately.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health data; anonymous usage statistics only use a hashed random identifier (section 9). Sunrise and sunset in the meadow are estimated from your device's time zone setting, not from location services.
  • The App does not use the microphone or camera; the background music is a built-in track.
  • No ads and no third-party advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties.

8. Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device (except files you exported and iOS system backups, which you manage yourself). To have feedback emails you sent deleted, just write to us.

Deleting the App does not remove data stored in iCloud. To delete Bunbit's data from iCloud: iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Bunbit → Delete (other devices that still have sync on are cleared too).

9. Anonymous usage statistics

By default, the App sends anonymous usage statistics so the developer can see which features are used and decide what to improve next. They are processed by TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service with servers in the EU.

  • What is sent: which features are used (for example opening the App, finishing a review, or the kind of habit created), plus device model, system version, App version and language. Counts are only sent as rough ranges (for example "2–3 habits").
  • What is never sent: habit names, numbers, review entries, goals, rabbit names, or any text or dates you enter.
  • The identifier is a random code generated on your device and then hashed. It is not linked to your identity, Apple Account or other apps, and is never used for tracking or advertising.
  • To turn it off: in the App, go to Settings → About & help and switch off "Anonymous usage statistics". Sending stops immediately, and anything not yet sent is discarded.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated.
