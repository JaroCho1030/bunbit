Bunbit 習慣兔 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 12 日　｜　English version below

Bunbit（習慣兔，以下稱「本 App」）是一個習慣養成 App。我們重視你的隱私，這份政策說明本 App 會用到哪些資料、資料放在哪裡。

一句話版本

你的習慣、打卡、檢討與目標資料只存在你自己的裝置上；開發者不會收集、也看不到這些資料。本 App 不需要註冊帳號，沒有廣告，也不會追蹤你。

1. 你在 App 裡輸入的資料

你建立的習慣、打卡紀錄、檢討內容、目標與兔子的相關資料，都儲存在你的 iPhone 或 iPad 本機，不會傳送到開發者的伺服器。本 App 沒有自己的伺服器，也不需要註冊帳號。

2. 通知

若你允許，本 App 會在你的裝置上排定提醒通知。通知完全在本機運作，不經過任何伺服器。你可以隨時在 iOS 的「設定」中關閉。

3. 匯出與備份

你可以把資料匯出成 CSV 或完整備份檔。檔案由你決定存放或分享的位置，開發者無法取得。

4. 購買

「Bunbit Plus」等付費項目透過 Apple 的 App Store 處理。付款資訊由 Apple 處理，本 App 與開發者不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。

5. 意見回饋

你在設定中點選「意見回饋」時，會開啟系統的寫信畫面，收件人與版本資訊會先填好，內容由你決定是否寄出。信件會附上 App 版本、iOS 版本、裝置型號與語言，方便我們釐清問題；不會附上任何習慣或打卡資料。你寄出的信件內容（包含你的信箱位址）會由開發者收到，僅用於回覆與改善 App。

6. TestFlight 測試版

若你透過 TestFlight 測試，Apple 會依其隱私政策提供測試者的電子郵件、當機紀錄與你在 TestFlight 中提交的回饋給開發者。這些由 Apple 的服務處理，本 App 本身不另外收集。

7. 我們不做的事

  • 不收集你的位置、聯絡人、相片、健康資料或裝置識別碼。
  • 不放廣告，不使用第三方廣告或追蹤工具。
  • 不出售、不出租、不與第三方分享你的個人資料。

8. 未來可能的變動

之後版本可能加入 iCloud 同步，以及匿名的使用統計。若加入，iCloud 資料會存放在你自己的 iCloud 帳號（Apple CloudKit 私人資料庫），開發者無法讀取；匿名統計不會連結到你的身分，並會提供關閉的選項。屆時我們會先更新這份政策。

9. 兒童

本 App 並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

10. 政策更新

政策有變動時，會更新本頁並修改「最後更新」日期。

11. 聯絡我們

對隱私有任何問題，請寄信到：snwjaro@gmail.com

Bunbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Bunbit (the "App") is a habit-building app. This policy explains what data the App uses and where it lives.

In short

Your habits, check-ins, reviews and goals stay on your own device. The developer does not collect or see them. The App requires no account, shows no ads, and does not track you.

1. Data you enter

Habits, check-ins, review entries, goals and rabbit data are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer. The App has no backend and no sign-up.

2. Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device and never pass through a server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

3. Export and backup

You can export your data as CSV or a full backup file. You decide where it is saved or shared; the developer cannot access it.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases such as Bunbit Plus are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details; neither the App nor the developer receives your card or payment information.

5. Feedback

When you tap "Feedback" in Settings, the system mail composer opens with the recipient and diagnostic info pre-filled, and you choose whether to send it. The message includes the App version, iOS version, device model and language, and never your habit or check-in data. If you send it, the developer receives the message and your email address, used only to reply and improve the App.

6. TestFlight beta

If you test through TestFlight, Apple may provide the developer with your email address, crash reports and feedback you submit through TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy. The App does not collect these separately.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or device identifiers.
  • No ads and no third-party advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties.

8. Possible future changes

Future versions may add iCloud sync and anonymous usage statistics. iCloud data would live in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database) and be unreadable to the developer; anonymous statistics would not be linked to your identity and would come with an off switch. This policy will be updated before such changes.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated.
