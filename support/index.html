最後更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日　｜　English version below

聯絡我們

有問題、建議或發現錯誤，寫信到 bunbitmail@gmail.com，或在 App 的「設定 → 關於與說明 → 意見回饋」寄信（會自動附上 App 版本與裝置型號，方便我們找問題；不會附上你的習慣資料）。開發者只有一個人，通常幾天內回覆。

常見問題

我的資料存在哪裡？

只存在你自己的 iPhone 或 iPad 上；打開 iCloud 同步（習慣兔 Plus）時，也存在你自己的 iCloud 帳號。開發者沒有伺服器，看不到你的資料。細節見隱私權政策。

換手機、重裝 App 要怎麼把資料帶過去？

「設定 → 資料 → 備份」存一個備份檔（放到 iCloud 雲碟或 AirDrop 到新手機），在新手機「設定 → 資料 → 從備份還原」就好。有習慣兔 Plus 的話，兩台都打開 iCloud 同步也可以。

買了習慣兔 Plus，換手機後沒有解鎖？

用同一個 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，到付費說明畫面（設定 → 資料 → iCloud 同步，或設定 → 使用介面 → App 圖示）按「恢復購買」。家人共享群組裡的成員也用同一個按鈕。

補登昨天的打卡，兔子為什麼沒長大？

草只給當天打的卡（和提前完成的）；補登昨天、前天照樣算進習慣化強度與完成率，只是不長草、兔子沒有經驗。兔子只會因為你做到而變好，漏做不會有任何懲罰。

提醒通知沒有出現？

到 iPhone「設定 → 通知 → Bunbit」確認允許通知，再到 App 的「設定 → 習慣、目標、與檢討」看每日彙整與檢討日提醒有沒有打開；每個習慣自己的提醒在編輯習慣的頁面。專注模式與排程摘要也可能延後通知。

怎麼把習慣兔的資料全部刪掉？

刪除 App 就會刪掉裝置上的資料。iCloud 上的另外在「iPhone 設定 → Apple 帳號 → iCloud → 管理帳號儲存空間 → Bunbit → 刪除」。

可以匯出嗎？

可以。「設定 → 資料 → 匯出 CSV」匯出全部習慣與打卡紀錄，或用「備份」存完整的備份檔。

Bunbit Support

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Contact

Questions, ideas or bugs: write to bunbitmail@gmail.com, or use Settings → About & help → Send feedback in the app (it attaches the app version and device model to help us find the problem, never your habit data). Bunbit is made by one person; replies usually take a few days.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own iPhone or iPad, and in your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync (Bunbit Plus). There is no developer server, and the developer can't see your data. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Settings → Data → Back up saves a backup file (put it in iCloud Drive or AirDrop it to the new phone), then Settings → Data → Restore from backup on the new phone. With Bunbit Plus you can also turn on iCloud sync on both devices.

I bought Bunbit Plus but it's locked on my new phone

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open the Bunbit Plus page (Settings → Data → iCloud sync, or Settings → Interface → App icon) and tap Restore purchase. Family Sharing members use the same button.

I caught up on yesterday's check-in, why didn't my bunny grow?

Grass only grows for check-ins made on the day (and for days completed early). Catch-ups still count toward habit strength and completion; they just don't grow grass. Bunnies only get better because you showed up, and missing a day never hurts them.

Reminders aren't showing up

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Bunbit, then in the app Settings → Habits, goals & reviews for the daily summary and review-day reminders; each habit's own reminder is on its edit page. Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can also delay notifications.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app removes everything on the device. For iCloud: iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Bunbit → Delete.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Data → Export CSV exports all habits and check-ins, or use Back up for a full backup file.
